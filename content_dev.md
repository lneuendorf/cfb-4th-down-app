# About the Site

This website is a tool for exploring college football team and coach tendencies on fourth down decision making. All fourth down recommendations are generated by a machine learning decision engine that evaluates the expected win probability of each available option.

For every fourth down play, the engine compares the expected win probability of going for it, punting, and attempting a field goal. The recommended decision is the one that maximizes expected win probability, accounting for both the value and likelihood of each possible outcome. Model inputs include game state, weather, stadium elevation, pregame point spread, and pregame team Elo ratings.

## Site Overview

### Teams
The **Teams** tab allows you to explore team-level fourth down tendencies for a selected season or range of seasons. This includes how often teams follow recommendations and the cumulative win probability impact of their decisions.

### Coaches
The **Coaches** tab focuses on coaching tendencies. You can examine how individual coaches approach fourth down decisions, how their behavior changes over time, and how they compare to their peers.

### Plays
The **Plays** tab provides play-level detail for every fourth down decision. You can filter by team, season, and optionally week, and inspect:
- Team and opponent Elo ratings at the time of the play
- Game state including score, quarter, time remaining, down, distance, and yards to goal
- Expected win probability for each decision
- The model recommendation, the actual decision made, and the play description

This view can be used to identify the best and worst decisions by a team, or to sanity check whether a given decision aligned with the model recommendation.

### Analysis
The **Analysis** tab contains longer-form writeups on the models powering the decision engine, including:
- Win probability model
- Fourth down conversion probability model
- Field goal make probability model
- Punt yards to goal model

It also includes analysis articles examining team-level decision making in the 2025 season and coaching tendencies toward aggressive or conservative behavior.

## Key Metrics

Across the **Teams** and **Coaches** tabs, several summary metrics are visualized:

**Go-For-It Rate When Recommended**
The percentage of fourth down plays where a team or coach goes for it when the model recommends doing so. A recommendation to go for it occurs when going for it has higher expected win probability than both punting and attempting a field goal.

**Win Probability Lost Per Season**
The total expected win probability lost over a season when a team or coach chooses to punt or kick a field goal despite going for it having higher expected win probability. For example, if going for it yields 53 percent expected win probability and a field goal yields 50 percent, the decision of kicking a field goal results in a 3 percent win probability loss.

**Go-For-It Rate Over Time**
A longitudinal view of how fourth down aggressiveness has changed from 2013 to the present, highlighting trends toward more aggressive or conservative decision making.
